Privacy Policy — Cape Command

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What Cape Command collects

Cape Command does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or photos.

Your game progress is stored only on your device. Cape Command does not sync your progress to iCloud or to any server.

Advertising

Cape Command is free and offers no in-app purchases. The only advertising in the game is optional rewarded ads, shown only on the Boosts screen and only when you choose to watch one.

Those ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, usage data, and diagnostics to serve ads, as described in Google's published SDK disclosure. Ads in Cape Command are configured as child-directed using age-restricted treatment (Google's Tag for Age Treatment, set to "child") with a general-audiences (G) content rating, so ads are non-personalized and no advertising identifier is used for tracking. Cape Command does not ask for tracking permission.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not offer in-app purchases. The game contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no links from gameplay to external websites.

Children

Cape Command is designed as a kid-friendly game. It collects no personal information from players of any age, and its advertising is configured as child-directed and non-personalized as described above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: capecommand.support@gmail.com.

This policy covers Cape Command as distributed on the App Store. If the game changes how it handles data, this page will be updated.
